Our Products

Nurturing Nature – Premium Agricultural Products from Sunny Fields.

Ground Pepper Powder

Sun-dried and ground pepper processed under hygienic conditions. Delivers strong aroma and long shelf life. Available in 1kg packs and 25kg bags.

Turmeric Powder

Turmeric powder is a vibrant, golden spice ground from the dried root of the Curcuma longa plant, a relative of ginger, known for its earthy, slightly bitter flavor and potent anti-inflammatory compound called curcumin.

Ginger Powder

Ginger powder is a finely ground, golden-beige spice from dried ginger roots, known for its warm, pungent, spicy flavor and strong aroma.

Dried Hibiscus Flowers

Carefully harvested and sun-dried to preserve natural red color and tangy flavor. Ideal for teas and beverages.

Dried Ginger Flakes

dehydrated, thinly sliced pieces of ginger root, offering a convenient, shelf-stable form of the spice with its characteristic warm, spicy, and pungent flavor, used to add zest to cooking, baking, teas, and remedies without needing to peel or grate fresh ginger.

Tigernuts

High-grade organic tigernuts, available fresh or dehydrated. Rich in fiber and perfect for food manufacturing.
